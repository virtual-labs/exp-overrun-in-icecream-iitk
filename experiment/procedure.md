Procedure <br>
Requirements (Instruments, Chemicals & Other)<br>
1.	Beaker<br>
2.	Graduated cylinders (200 ml)<br>
3.	Spatula<br>
4.	Water Bath<br>
5.	Funnel<br>

Procedure  <br>
1.	Take a 100 ml frozen ice cream cup. <br>
2.	Empty the frozen ice cream to a 200 ml beaker using spatula.<br>
3.	Set the temperature of water bath to 49℃. <br>
4.	Place the beaker in water bath and let it stand till all the ice cream melts.<br>
5.	Take a 250 ml measuring cylinder. Place a funnel on it and transfer the melted ice- cream into it.
6.	Add 10 ml water into the beaker. Shake it thoroughly and transfer rinsing water to the measuring cylinder.<br>
7.	Again add 10 ml water to the beaker, shake it thoroughly and transfer the rinsing water to the measuring cylinder. <br>
8.	Record the final volume (V1) in the measuring cylinder.<br>
9.	Calculations:<br>
Volume of melted ice-cream (ml)= V1-10-10<br>
Volume of frozen ice-cream (ml)= 200<br>
