### About the Experiment 

In this experiment, the quality of ice cream will be evaluated by determining its overrun, which is the measure of air incorporated during the freezing process. Overrun is an important quality parameter that affects the texture, body, palatability, and economic value of ice cream. The experiment involves melting a known volume of frozen ice cream and measuring the final volume after removing the incorporated air. This helps in calculating the percentage of overrun present in the sample. Proper overrun ensures desirable softness, smoothness, and uniform structure, while excessive or low overrun may adversely affect product quality. This experiment provides practical knowledge of ice cream quality evaluation and helps in understanding the role of air incorporation in frozen dairy products.
